How the record works

The board can go down.

A call opens with the live BTC price written onto it. When the analyst closes the position — or when the candle runs out, whichever comes first — the price is read again and the call is scored. Nobody types the result in afterwards.

A move too small to matter counts as flat rather than a win, and nobody is ranked on the leaderboard until they have five graded calls, so one lucky call cannot sit above a long record. Check any analyst yourself with /picks record.
